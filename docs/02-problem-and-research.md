# 02 — Problem & Research

## 1. Research Approach

This project began from a lived experience navigating Sarathi without an agent.

The research combines:

- personal experience
- supplied screenshots
- supplied application documents
- observed Sarathi journey
- service documentation
- UX pattern research
- design hypotheses

This is exploratory product research, not a statistically representative study.
