# 10 — Validation Plan

## Objective

Validate whether the redesigned journey improves understanding and completion of the endorsement process.

---

# Research Questions

## Q1

Can users identify the prerequisite before starting?

## Q2

Can users understand their current state?

## Q3

Can users tell whether they need to act?

## Q4

Can users identify the next step?

## Q5

Can users understand waiting states?

## Q6

Can users recover from a blocked state?

## Q7

Can users complete the journey without external help?

---

# Participants

Ideal participants:

- people who have used Sarathi
- people who have applied for a Driving Licence
- people who have added a vehicle class
- people who have attempted self-service government applications

Include both:

- experienced digital users
- less confident digital users

---

# Tasks

## Task 1

"You already have an LMV licence. Add MCWG."

Observe whether the user identifies the LL prerequisite.

---

## Task 2

"Your application has been submitted."

Ask:

"What would you do next?"

Observe whether the user understands the waiting state.

---

## Task 3

"The application has moved forward."

Ask:

"What changed?"

Observe whether the notification communicates meaning.

---

## Task 4

"The journey is blocked."

Ask:

"What would you do?"

Observe recovery comprehension.

---

# Metrics

## Completion

Percentage of users who complete the journey.

## Prerequisite comprehension

Percentage who correctly identify the prerequisite.

## Next-action clarity

Percentage who correctly identify the next action.

## Recovery success

Percentage who successfully recover from a blocked state.

## Confidence

Ask:

"How confident are you that you know what to do next?"

Use a 1–5 scale.

---

# Qualitative Questions

Ask:

- What were you expecting here?
- What would you do if this were real?
- What does this status mean to you?
- Who do you think needs to act?
- What would you do next?
- Is anything missing?
- What would make you trust this information?

---

# Comparative Test

If possible, compare:

### Current-style flow

Technical status + disconnected transactions.

versus:

### Proposed flow

Persistent journey + actionable status.

Measure:

- completion
- comprehension
- confidence
- time to next action

---

# Success Criteria

The design is promising if users can consistently:

1. identify prerequisites
2. understand current state
3. identify responsibility
4. identify next action
5. recover from blockers

---

# Research Limitation

The current prototype is not yet validated with a statistically representative population.

Any observed improvements should be treated as directional.

---

# Future Research

Test across:

- different states
- different RTOs
- first-time applicants
- endorsement applicants
- renewal applicants
- users with and without agents
