# 06 — Content & Microcopy

## Content Principle

Use plain language.

Government terminology may be preserved where necessary, but it should be explained.

---

# Status Framework

Every important state follows:

### WHAT HAPPENED

### WHAT IS HAPPENING

### WHO NEEDS TO ACT

### WHAT DO I NEED TO DO

### WHAT HAPPENS NEXT

---

# Waiting

## Avoid

"Application under processing at RTO level."

## Prefer

"Your application is being reviewed by the RTO."

"You don't need to do anything right now."

---

# Action Required

## Headline

Your next step is ready

## Body

Your application has moved forward.

You can now continue to the next stage.

## CTA

Continue

---

# Prerequisite Missing

## Headline

You're not ready to continue yet

## Body

You need an MCWG Learner's Licence before continuing with this journey.

## CTA

Apply for MCWG Learner's Licence

---

# Eligibility Waiting

## Headline

You're not ready for the next stage yet

## Body

Your Learner's Licence is active, but the eligibility period is still in progress.

## Supporting

We'll show you when the next step becomes available.

---

# Stalled

## Headline

Your application hasn't progressed

## Body

Your application is still showing the same stage.

## Supporting

Last updated: [synthetic date]

## CTA

Review application

---

# Blocked

## Headline

Your journey is blocked

## Body

We found a requirement that isn't complete yet.

## Supporting

Here's what you need to do to continue.

---

# Completed

## Headline

MCWG has been added to your licence

## Body

Your licence journey is complete.

---

# Notification

## Headline

Your next step is ready

## Body

Your application has moved to the next stage.

## CTA

Continue

---

# AI Explanation

## User

What does "Approval of LL by Licensing Authority" mean?

## AI

It means your Learner's Licence application is currently being reviewed by the licensing authority.

You don't need to take any action at this stage.

We'll let you know when your next step becomes available.

---

# Tone

Use:

- calm
- direct
- respectful
- reassuring
- concise

Avoid:

- bureaucratic
- patronising
- overly casual
- alarmist
- marketing language

---

# Words to Prefer

"You're ready"

"Nothing required right now"

"Your next step"

"Here's what happens next"

"You need to"

"We're reviewing"

"Your journey is complete"

---

# Words to Avoid

"Proceed accordingly"

"Application under process"

"Applicant is requested to"

"Kindly"

"Hereinafter"

"Processing at department level"

unless the official term itself needs to be displayed.
